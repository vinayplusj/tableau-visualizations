Digital Analytics Dashboard for Lead Generation Business

A Tableau dashboard focused on lead generation performance, channel contribution, and executive-friendly KPI tracking.

Project overview

This dashboard is designed to help business and marketing stakeholders monitor lead generation performance in one place. It brings together high-level KPIs and supporting views so users can quickly understand trend direction, channel contribution, and overall acquisition performance.

Business use case

Lead generation teams often need a simple reporting layer that can be reviewed regularly without requiring deep technical knowledge. This dashboard is built to support that need by presenting performance in a compact and decision-friendly format.

Intended audience

  • Marketing managers
  • Digital analytics teams
  • Business stakeholders
  • Performance reporting teams

What this dashboard highlights

  • Lead volume and trend over time
  • Channel and campaign contribution
  • Conversion-related performance signals
  • Executive-friendly KPI monitoring

What this project demonstrates

  • KPI-focused dashboard design
  • Clear visual communication for business users
  • Tableau-based reporting and presentation
  • Practical digital analytics storytelling
