# Digital Analytics Dashboard for Lead Generation Business

## Overview

This Tableau dashboard teamplate is designed to track lead generation performance and support business and marketing decision-making.

It brings together key lead and acquisition metrics into a format that is easy for stakeholders to review regularly.

## Business use case

This dashboard is useful for teams that need visibility into:
- lead generation trends
- channel contribution
- campaign effectiveness
- conversion-related outcomes
- high-level performance monitoring

## Intended audience

- Marketing managers
- Digital analytics teams
- Business leaders
- Performance reporting teams
